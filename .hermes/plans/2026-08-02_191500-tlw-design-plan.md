## 2. Game Concept: "The Lost World"

### 2.1 Elevator Pitch

**The Lost World** is a single-player browser game where you discover, restore, and nurture a forgotten ecosystem. Starting from a single cleared patch of land, you expand outward into procedurally-revealed biomes — each with unique flora, fauna, geology, and ancient secrets. You are not just a gardener; you are a **world-restorer**.

### 2.2 Differentiation from "The Lost Garden"

| The Lost Garden | The Lost World |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Single garden property | Expansive world map with multiple biomes |
| Pre-defined zones | Procedurally-revealed / fog-of-war exploration |
| Plant + garden focus | Full ecosystem: geology, weather, wildlife, archaeology |
| Fixed isometric view | Dynamic camera: zoom, pan, biome transitions |
| Restoration of neglect | Discovery of the unknown |
| Cottage garden aesthetic | Broader palette: from lush jungle to arid mesa to frozen tundra |

### 2.3 Core Pillars

1. **Exploration** — Reveal the map. Every cleared area uncovers neighbouring terrain. Fog-of-war system.
2. **Restoration** — Clear, repair, rebuild. Transform ruined biomes into thriving ecosystems.
3. **Cultivation** — Plant, grow, crossbreed. Each biome has native species you can propagate and harvest.
4. **Discovery** — Find ancient artifacts, lost species, geological wonders. Fill your Field Journal.
5. **Connection** — Wildlife returns as you restore. Build habitats, attract species, complete ecosystem chains.
6. **Homesteading** — Late-game: unlock farming plots, build cottages, keep animals, craft from harvests. The cottagecore payoff.

### 2.4 Player Fantasy

You are the **Custodian** — the last steward of a world that fell into dormancy. An ancient map, found in a forgotten archive, marks the location of the Lost World. Your airship drops you at the edge of the known map. From there, it's just you, your tools, and a world waiting to breathe again.

## 3. World Design

### 3.1 Biome System

Each biome is a distinct ecological zone with unique characteristics:

| Biome | Palette | Flora | Fauna | Hazards | Special |
|-------|---------|-------|-------|---------|---------|
| **Verdant Valley** | Emerald greens, gold sunlight | Wildflowers, fruit trees, herbs | Bees, robins, hedgehogs | Brambles, weeds | Starting biome |
| **Sunken Marsh** | Teal, murky green, purple mist | Mangroves, fungi, luminescent plants | Frogs, dragonflies, egrets | Thick mud, toxic spores | Bioluminescent discoveries |
| **Crystal Caverns** | Deep blue, violet, silver glint | Crystal-lichen, cave moss | Bats, glow-worms, blind fish | Cave-ins, darkness | Geodes, rare minerals |
| **Scorched Mesa** | Terracotta, amber, dusty rose | Cacti, succulents, desert bloom | Lizards, hawks, foxes | Drought, sandstorms | Fossil beds, ancient ruins |
| **Frozen Tundra** | Ice blue, white, pale lavender | Frost flowers, evergreen shrubs | Arctic hares, snow owls, caribou | Blizzards, thin ice | Preserved specimens, ice cores |
| **Canopy Jungle** | Deep greens, tropical brights | Orchids, bromeliads, giant ferns | Parrots, monkeys, jaguars | Dense undergrowth, storms | Lost temple, rare epiphytes |
| **Coastal Reach** | Azure, sand, coral pink | Sea grasses, mangroves, kelp | Turtles, seals, seabirds | Tides, salt damage | Shipwrecks, tidal pools |

### 3.2 Map Structure

- Hex-grid based world map
- Start at one edge hex (Verdant Valley)
- Adjacent hexes revealed gradually (fog-of-war)
- Biome transitions are semi-procedural — hand-designed seed with procedural detail
- Each biome has 3–5 sub-zones (clearings, groves, ruins, habitats)
- World expands outward as player progresses

### 3.3 Zone States

Every sub-zone cycles through states:
```
Dormant (fogged/locked) → Overgrown (needs clearing) → Restored (functional) → Thriving (bonus yields)
```

## 4. Game Systems

### 4.1 Restoration System

Inherited and expanded from the reference:

- **Clear**: Remove overgrowth, debris, hazards (costs Energy + Tool durability)
- **Repair**: Fix broken structures, paths, bridges (costs Materials + Time)
- **Restore**: Replant native species, reintroduce water (costs Seeds + Water)
- **Thrive**: Maintain ecosystem balance for bonus rewards (ongoing)

Restoration queue: up to 5 concurrent timed actions. Premium unlocks parallel queues.

### 4.2 Planting & Cultivation

- **Seed collection**: Gathered from exploration, rewards, market
- **Planting**: Biome-appropriate species only (cactus won't grow in tundra)
- **Growth timers**: Real-time, varies by species (minutes to days)
- **Crossbreeding**: Combine species for hybrid variants (rare/completionist)
- **Quality**: Affected by soil health, companion planting, weather
- **Journal**: Complete botanical entries with Latin names, illustrations, growing tips

### 4.3 Wildlife & Ecosystem

- **Species discovery**: Wildlife appears as biomes are restored
- **Habitat building**: Construct birdhouses, bee hotels, ponds, burrows
- **Adventure paths**: Per-species quest chains (like the Honey Bee path in the reference)
- **Ecosystem chains**: Species depend on each other — restore plants to attract insects to attract birds to attract predators
- **Sightings log**: Real-time activity feed of wildlife in your world
- **Collection**: Field guide with scientific names, illustrations, behaviours

### 4.4 Archaeology & Discovery

- **Artifact fragments**: Found during clearing/restoration
- **Reconstruction**: Combine fragments to reveal ancient lore, blueprints, map pieces
- **Lore**: The story of what happened to the Lost World, told through discovered journals, carvings, fossils
- **Museum**: Reconstructed artifacts displayed in your Custodian's Lodge

### 4.5 Homesteading & Farming (Late-Game Unlock)

Once a biome reaches "Thriving" status and the player has achieved sufficient Custodian level, farmland plots become unlockable. This is the cottagecore payoff — the world is no longer just being restored, it's being *lived in*.

- **Farmland plots**: Clear and till land for row crops, orchards, and pastures
- **Crops at scale**: Larger planting areas, higher yields, harvest cycles
- **Animal husbandry**: Keep chickens, goats, sheep, bees — not just wild species but domesticated companions
- **Crafting**: Turn harvests into goods — preserves, baked goods, cheeses, textiles, pottery
- **Cottage building**: Construct and decorate your Custodian's Cottage (separate from the Lodge). Customise furnishings, garden layout, fences, pathways
- **Seasonal rhythms**: Crop rotation, winter preparation, spring planting festivals
- **Cottagecore activities**: Foraging baskets, drying herbs, arranging wildflowers, baking bread, spinning wool — low-pressure ambient activities with small rewards

Farming is gated behind restoration — you can't farm land you haven't healed first. This creates a natural progression: explore → restore → cultivate → homestead.

### 4.6 Economy

| Currency | Source | Use |
|----------|--------|-----|
| **Coins** | Selling harvests, crafted goods, completing quests | Buying seeds, tools, decorations, cottage furnishings |
| **Energy (Leaves)** | Time-based regeneration, consuming food | Actions (clear, plant, build, craft) |
| **Water** | Rain collection, wells, rivers, crafted irrigation | Irrigation, habitat maintenance, animal care |
| **Harmony** | Ecosystem balance, thriving zones, biodiversity | Unlocking new biomes, special abilities, rare seeds |
| **Materials** | Clearing, foraging, mining | Building, repairing, crafting |
| **Gems** | Rare discoveries, achievements, completing quest chains, (optional purchase) | Cottage decorations, cosmetic items, visual customisations |

> **Core gameplay is free.** Gems are earnable through play. Optional purchase exists only for cosmetic cottage decorations — wallpapers, furniture skins, garden ornaments, clothing for the Custodian. Nothing that affects gameplay balance.

### 4.7 Energy System

Energy (Leaves) is the action-gating currency. Every meaningful action — clearing, planting, building, crafting — costs a Leaf cost. The design intent is gentle pacing, not aggressive monetisation.

**Capacity:**
- Base cap: 100 Leaves (increases with Custodian level, up to ~300)
- Visible as a leaf-count in the HUD

**Regeneration:**
- Passive: 1 Leaf every 3 minutes (20/hour, cap refills in ~5 hours from empty)
- Active: consuming food (cooked meals = large restore, raw ingredients = small restore)
- Bonus: sleeping at the Cottage overnight grants full restore + temporary "Well-Rested" buff (actions cost 20% less for 2 hours of play)

**Costs (baseline, reducible by tool upgrades and buffs):**
- Clear/Chop: 10 Leaves
- Plant seed: 5 Leaves
- Water: 3 Leaves
- Build structure: 15–30 Leaves
- Craft item: 8–12 Leaves
- Explore new hex: 20 Leaves
- Inspect/study: 2 Leaves

**Upgrades:**
- Tool quality reduces energy cost per action type
- Well-Rested buff (from cottage sleep)
- Consumable energy boosts (rare teas, honey)
- Harmony milestones grant permanent energy cost reductions in that biome

**Design constraint:** A typical 20-minute play session should let the player complete 3–5 meaningful actions before energy depletes, with food or sleep restoring enough to continue if desired. No "pay for energy" mechanics.

### 4.8 Progression

- **Custodian Level**: XP from all activities. Unlocks new tools, biome access, queue slots.
- **Biome Mastery**: Per-biome star rating (1–5) based on restoration %, species diversity, wildlife health
- **Journal Completion**: % of species discovered, artifacts found, lore uncovered
- **Seasonal Events**: In-game seasons change visuals, available species, and special events

## 6. Image Generation Strategy (Imagen 4)

### 6.1 Model

**Imagen 4** (`imagen-4.0-generate-001`) via the Google GenAI SDK. Imagen 4 produces high-fidelity, realistic images from text prompts — we constrain the output to "realistic but stylised" via prompt engineering rather than relying on an inherently stylised model. All generated images include a SynthID watermark.

Key Imagen 4 capabilities:
- High-fidelity photorealism that we steer toward stylised realism
- English-only prompts
- `numberOfImages` parameter (up to 4 per call)
- Photography modifier support (camera, lighting, lens, film)
- Text-in-image support (<25 chars per phrase)
- Parameterised prompt support for dynamic generation

Budget approach: generate freely, and Dom will adjust if costs escalate. No hard cap at this stage.

### 6.2 Prompt Architecture

Based on the [Imagen prompt guide](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/imagen#imagen-prompt-guide):

#### Base Prompt Template
```
[STYLE ANCHOR] [SUBJECT] in a [BIOME] setting. [COMPOSITION details]. 
[LIGHTING description]. [COLOUR PALETTE reference]. 
[QUALITY MODIFIERS]. Semi-realistic digital painting, stylised realism, 
cottagecore game art for a cozy restoration sim.
```

#### Style Anchors (by content type)

| Content Type | Style Anchor |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Zone art** | "Isometric 2.5D semi-realistic painting of..." |
| **Wildlife card** | "Semi-realistic field guide specimen illustration of..." |
| **Item icon** | "Clean game UI icon, flat with soft shadows, of..." |
| **Biome panorama** | "Wide landscape semi-realistic painting, cottagecore aesthetic, of..." |
| **UI decoration** | "Ornamental botanical illustration with gold leaf accents of..." |
| **Artifact** | "Archaeological specimen illustration on aged parchment of..." |
| **Cottagecore scene** | "Warm domestic scene, cottagecore interior, stylised realism of..." |

#### Photography Modifiers

Used to steer Imagen 4 away from sterile photorealism and toward a painterly, curated look:

```
Camera: tiltshift, wide angle, aerial view, close-up
Lighting: golden hour, soft diffused natural light, warm afternoon sun, overcast softbox
Lens: shallow depth of field, bokeh background, 35mm
Film: warm tones, slightly muted colours, Kodak Portra, polaroid
Atmosphere: morning mist, dappled sunlight through leaves, candlelit interior
```

#### Negative Prompt Anchors

Always include these signals to avoid photorealism:
```
stylised realism, painterly texture, not photorealistic, illustrated feel, 
game art quality, warm curated palette
```

### 6.3 Parameterised Prompts

For UI-driven generation where players can influence output:

```
A {detail_level} semi-realistic illustration of a {species} in a {biome} habitat.
{season} season, {time_of_day} lighting. {style_modifier}.
Cottagecore game art, stylised realism, warm parchment tones.
```

This allows dynamic content generation: e.g., "A highly detailed semi-realistic illustration of a Snow Owl in a Frozen Tundra habitat. Winter season, twilight lighting. Painterly texture."

### 6.4 Iterative Refinement Workflow

1. **Seed prompt** — short, captures the core idea with style anchors
2. **First pass** — generate 4 variants, select best
3. **Refine** — adjust lighting, composition, colour specifics; strengthen stylisation language if output is too photorealistic
4. **Second pass** — generate 4 more with refined prompt
5. **Final select** — pick the best; minor manual touch-up if needed

### 6.5 Generation Pipeline

```
Game event (zone restored, species discovered, artifact found)
       ↓
Prompt template selected by content type
       ↓
Parameter injection (biome, species name, season, palette)
       ↓
Style anchors + negative anchors applied
       ↓
Imagen 4 API call (imagen-4.0-generate-001, 4 images)
       ↓
Best variant selected → cached → served to player → stored in asset library
```

All generated images include SynthID watermark (automatic).

### 7.3 Rendering Approach

The game world is **not a full 3D engine**. Instead:

1. **Background art** per zone is a single high-quality generated image (Imagen 4)
2. **Interactive elements** (plants, structures, wildlife) are overlaid as positioned sprites
3. **Fog-of-war** is CSS mask or Canvas alpha
4. **Transitions** between zone states (overgrown → restored → thriving) use crossfade between generated images
5. **World map** is a hex grid rendered on Canvas with biome tiles

This keeps the build simple (no WebGL requirement), performs well on mobile, and leverages AI image generation for the heavy visual lifting.

## 10. Key Design Decisions & Rationale

| Decision | Rationale |
|----------|-----------|
| **2.5D isometric, not full 3D** | Faster to build, better mobile perf, AI-generated art works better in 2.5D |
| **Hex grid world map** | Natural for exploration, familiar from strategy games, good fog-of-war |
| **Real-time timers** | Proven engagement loop from reference; naturally paces gameplay |
| **Six currencies** | Coins, Energy, Water, Harmony, Materials, Gems — each gates a different loop |
| **Biome-locked species** | Encourages exploration; prevents overwhelming player early |
| **AI-generated art as core** | Differentiator from asset-store games; infinite variety; prompt templates ensure consistency |
| **Offline-first PWA** | No server dependency for core loop; image generation is the only online requirement |
| **Single-player only** | Reduces scope; multiplayer adds complexity disproportionate to value for this genre |
| **Farming gated behind restoration** | Creates natural progression — you can't farm land you haven't healed; makes homesteading feel earned |
| **Realistic but stylised** | Imagen 4 is inherently photorealistic; prompt engineering constrains it to a warm, painterly cottagecore aesthetic |
| **Living game** | Post-launch biome additions, seasonal events, expanding world — designed for ongoing content |

## 11. Decisions Confirmed

All open questions resolved (2 August 2026):

1. **Image model**: Imagen 4 (`imagen-4.0-generate-001`). Realistic but stylised via prompt engineering.
2. **Visual style**: Cottagecore, semi-realistic, painterly — not sterile photorealism.
3. **Farming**: Late-game unlock, gated behind biome restoration. Full farming system with crops, animals, crafting, cottage building.
4. **Monetisation**: Gems earnable through play. Optional purchase for cosmetic cottage decorations only — wallpapers, furniture skins, garden ornaments, Custodian outfits. Zero gameplay impact.
5. **Energy**: Leaf-based energy system with passive regen (1/3min), food restore, and cottage sleep buff. ~3–5 actions per 20-min session. No pay-for-energy.
6. **Narrative**: Mix of environmental storytelling (discovered journals, carvings, fossils) and explicit questlines (adventure paths, restoration goals).
7. **Social**: Solo only for now. No multiplayer, no clubs, no leaderboards.
8. **Budget**: Generate freely with Imagen 4. Dom will monitor costs and adjust if needed.
9. **Scope**: Living game. Six biomes for initial launch; designed for post-launch expansion.
10. **Audio**: Future TODO. Ambient soundscapes per biome, gentle music. Not in scope for Phase 0–5.
